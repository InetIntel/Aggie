# KPI tracking

KPI tracking is under Settings. Admins can see everything. Team leaders can see their teams, and admins can grant others access.

It counts new accounts, logins, incidents created, and alerts/social posts linked to incidents or marked investigate/ignore.

Filter by date or team. “48 (12 shared)” means 12 of those 48 also count for another team you can see. The overall total counts them once. CSV exports keep the daily counts and coverage. Dates use UTC.

Teams are recorded when the action happens. Older activity stays under “Team not recorded.” Repeated links or flags count once per report per day; every successful login counts. Changing teams or undoing an action doesn't change past totals.
